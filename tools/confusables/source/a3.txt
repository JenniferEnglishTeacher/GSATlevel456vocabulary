- exhibition | n. | a public show of art or objects | 展覽 | show, display | | The art exhibition attracted more than ten thousand visitors. | 這場藝術展吸引了一萬多名參觀者。

@A illuminate / eliminate

@A immense / intense

@A incline / decline
